# Bahay Basics Online Shop: Customer Policy

Bahay Basics sells home and kitchen goods online through our Facebook page and Shopee store. We ship nationwide from Quezon City.

## Shipping
- Couriers: J&T Express for all orders. Lalamove same-day delivery is available within Metro Manila for orders placed before 12 noon, at the customer's cost.
- Delivery time: Metro Manila 2 to 3 business days. Luzon provinces 3 to 5 business days. Visayas and Mindanao 5 to 7 business days.
- Orders are packed and handed to the courier within 1 business day of payment confirmation.
- A tracking number is sent by SMS and Messenger once the parcel is handed to J&T.
- If an order has not moved for 3 business days, we file a follow-up ticket with J&T on the customer's behalf.

## Shipping fees
- Metro Manila: PHP 80.
- Luzon provinces: PHP 120.
- Visayas and Mindanao: PHP 150.
- Free shipping on orders of PHP 1,500 and above, anywhere in the Philippines.

## Payment
- Accepted: GCash, Maya, bank transfer (BPI, BDO), and cash on delivery (COD).
- COD is available for orders up to PHP 3,000 only.
- For GCash, Maya, and bank transfer, send a screenshot of the payment to our Messenger page. Payment is confirmed within 2 hours during store hours.
- We do not accept credit cards at this time.

## Order changes and cancellation
- Changes (item, color, quantity, address) are allowed only before the order ships.
- Daily cut-off for changes is 2 PM. Orders paid before 2 PM ship the same day and cannot be changed after cut-off.
- Cancellation is free before shipping. Prepaid orders are refunded in full.
- Once shipped, an order cannot be cancelled. The customer may file a return after receiving it.

## Returns and refunds
- Returns are accepted within 7 days of delivery.
- Items must be unused, in original packaging, with the order slip.
- Damaged or wrong items: send a photo within 48 hours of delivery. We cover return shipping and send a replacement or a full refund.
- Change of mind: customer pays return shipping. Refund excludes the original shipping fee.
- Refunds go back to the original payment method within 3 business days of receiving the returned item. COD orders are refunded by GCash.

## Warranty
- Small appliances (rice cookers, kettles, blenders, air fryers) have a 30-day replacement warranty for factory defects.
- The warranty does not cover damage from misuse, drops, or wrong voltage. All appliances are 220V.

## Products
- Product sizes, materials, and colors are listed on each product page.
- Restocks usually happen every 2 weeks. Customers can ask to be notified by Messenger.
- Bulk orders of 10 or more of the same item get 10% off. Message us for a quote.

## Store hours and contact
- Messenger replies: Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 6 PM. Messages on Sundays and holidays are answered the next business day.
- We do not take phone calls. All support is through Messenger and Shopee chat.

## Tone
- Reply in the customer's language: English, Filipino, or Taglish.
- Be warm and brief. Use "po" when the customer uses it.
- Always include the order ID in the reply when the customer gave one.
- Never promise a delivery date or refund timing that this policy does not support.
